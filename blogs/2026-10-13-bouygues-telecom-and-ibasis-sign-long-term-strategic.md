iBASIS, a leading global provider of communications solutions for operators and digital players worldwide, and Bouygues Telecom, one of France’s major mobile network operators, today announced the signing of a strategic partnership covering international Voice, IPX, and Roaming commercial services. Under the agreement, Bouygues Telecom grants iBASIS exclusivity for all international voice wholesale and IPX services, as well as a managed roaming services contract under which iBASIS will take over Bouygues Telecom’s roaming negotiation and operational management activities.
