By 2030, up to 95% of all new vehicles will be connected, according to McKinsey*. These aren’t just cars with navigation or apps. They are software-defined platforms on wheels, constantly exchanging data, receiving updates, transmitting diagnostics, and increasingly interacting with smart infrastructure and other vehicles.
